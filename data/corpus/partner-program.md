# Partner and Reseller Program

## Referral programme

Referral partners receive 15 percent of the first year's subscription value, paid 60 days after the customer's first invoice is settled. Referrals are attributed to the partner who registered the opportunity first.

## Resellers

Authorised resellers buy at a margin of 20 to 30 percent depending on volume and handle first-line support for their customers. Resellers cannot set prices below the published list price without written approval.
