# Subprocessors and Subcontractors

## Current subprocessors

Northwind Cloud uses Amazon Web Services for compute and storage, Cloudflare for CDN and DDoS protection, Stripe for payment processing, and SendGrid for transactional email. An up-to-date list with purpose and location is published on the trust portal.

## Change notification

Customers are notified at least 30 days before a new subprocessor is engaged and may object in writing. If an objection cannot be resolved, the customer may terminate the affected contract without penalty.
