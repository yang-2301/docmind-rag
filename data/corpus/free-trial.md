# Free Trial Terms

## Trial length

Every new workspace starts with a 14 day trial of the Business plan, including all features except SCIM provisioning and custom data residency. No credit card is required to start. At the end of the trial the workspace downgrades to a read-only state for 14 days before data is scheduled for deletion.

## Extensions

One 14 day extension is available on request through support. A second extension requires approval from an account manager and is granted only for active evaluation projects.
