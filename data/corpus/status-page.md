# Status Page and Maintenance Windows

## Status page

The status page at status.northwindcloud.example reports component-level health and incident history. Subscribers receive email or SMS updates. The status page is hosted outside the production infrastructure so that it remains available during an outage.

## Maintenance windows

Standard maintenance is scheduled on Sundays between 02:00 and 06:00 UTC and announced at least 72 hours in advance. Emergency maintenance may be performed with 2 hours notice. Time inside an announced maintenance window does not count against the SLA commitment.
