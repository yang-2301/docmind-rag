# Plans and Included Usage

## Plan tiers

Northwind Cloud is sold in four plans: Starter, Team, Business and Enterprise. Starter includes 2 seats, 50 GB of document storage and 1,000 API calls per month. Team includes 10 seats, 500 GB and 25,000 API calls. Business includes 50 seats, 2 TB and 250,000 API calls. Enterprise is quoted per contract and has no fixed seat cap. Seats and storage can be raised on any plan; API calls above the included amount are billed at overage rates.

## Overage pricing

Overage is charged per 1,000 calls. Starter pays 0.90 USD per 1,000 calls, Team pays 0.60 USD, Business pays 0.35 USD and Enterprise pays the rate in the order form. Storage overage is 0.08 USD per GB per month on every plan except Enterprise. Overage is calculated at the end of the billing period and never blocks API traffic; the account is not throttled for exceeding included usage.
