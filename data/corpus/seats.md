# Seats and License Management

## Adding and removing seats

Seats are billed per active member. Adding a seat mid-cycle prorates the charge to the end of the month. Removing a seat does not generate a credit; the seat becomes available for reuse until the period ends.

## Viewer seats

Viewer seats are read-only and cost 20 percent of a full seat on Team, Business and Enterprise. Starter has no viewer seats. Service accounts used for API access are free and do not count toward the seat limit.
