# Incident Response Process

## Lifecycle

Every incident follows five stages: detection, triage, mitigation, resolution and review. A public status update is posted within 30 minutes of a confirmed P1 and every 60 minutes afterwards until resolution. The incident channel is opened in the status page, not only in email.

## Post-incident review

A written post-incident review is published within 5 business days for any P1 and any incident that breached the SLA. Enterprise customers receive the review by email; other customers can request it from support. Reviews include a timeline, root cause and dated remediation items.
