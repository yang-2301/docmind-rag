# Disaster Recovery

## Targets

The recovery point objective is 15 minutes and the recovery time objective is 4 hours for the API and web application in the primary region. Cross-region failover is tested twice a year and the results are summarised in the trust portal.

## Customer responsibilities

Customers are responsible for their own integrations, credentials and any data exported outside Northwind. DR does not cover customer-side automation or third-party systems that lose connectivity.
