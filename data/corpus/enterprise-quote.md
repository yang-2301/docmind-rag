# Enterprise Quoting Process

## Steps

Enterprise pricing is quoted after a scoping call and a security review. Quotes are valid for 30 days. The process from scoping call to countersigned order form typically takes 3 to 6 weeks; security review is the most common source of delay.

## Custom terms

Enterprise contracts may override uptime commitment, payment terms, liability caps and data residency. Any clause in the order form overrides the public policy page for that customer.
