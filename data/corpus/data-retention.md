# Data Retention and Deletion

## Retention after cancellation

When a workspace is cancelled, the contents remain readable in a suspended state for 30 days so that the customer can export. After the 30 day grace period the workspace is queued for deletion and data is purged from primary storage within 7 days. Encrypted backups containing the data expire within 35 days after purge.

## Early deletion requests

A customer may request immediate deletion instead of waiting for the grace period by opening a ticket with the category Privacy, then Erasure. Deletion is irreversible and requires written confirmation from an account owner. Requests from a non-owner are refused.

## Legal holds

Data covered by a legal hold or a lawful preservation order is exempt from deletion until the hold is lifted, and the customer is notified unless prohibited by law.
