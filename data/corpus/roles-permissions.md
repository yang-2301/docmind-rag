# Roles and Permissions

## Built-in roles

Owner can transfer ownership and delete the workspace; there is exactly one owner. Admin manages members, billing and integrations. Editor creates and modifies documents. Viewer reads documents and cannot export. Billing is a separate role that sees invoices but no content.

## Custom roles

Enterprise workspaces can define custom roles from a permission list of 34 individual permissions. Custom roles cannot grant permissions the workspace plan does not include.
