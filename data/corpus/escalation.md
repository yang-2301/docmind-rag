# Escalation Matrix

## Internal escalation

If a P1 ticket is not acknowledged within 30 minutes the on-call engineer is paged. If it is not resolved or mitigated within 2 hours the incident is escalated to the Engineering Manager, and after 4 hours to the VP of Engineering. Enterprise customers may request a written status update every 60 minutes during a P1.

## Customer escalation

A customer who believes a ticket is not progressing may escalate to their account manager, or to support-escalation@northwindcloud.example. Escalations are reviewed within one business day and a named owner is assigned.
