# GDPR and Data Processing Addendum

## Roles and legal basis

For customer content, Northwind Cloud acts as a processor and the customer is the controller. For account and billing data Northwind Cloud is an independent controller. Processing relies on the performance of the contract and, for marketing email, on consent.

## Signing the DPA

The Data Processing Addendum is available from the trust portal and can be executed by an account owner in Settings, Legal without a sales conversation. EU standard contractual clauses are pre-signed and included with the DPA.

## Data location

Customers can choose the EU (Frankfurt) or US (Virginia) region at workspace creation. The region cannot be changed after the first document is uploaded; migrating regions requires a new workspace and a support-assisted export.
