# Onboarding and Implementation

## Standard onboarding

A new Business or Enterprise workspace receives a kickoff call within 3 business days of signature, a data migration workshop in week one, and a go-live review in week four. The standard implementation takes 30 days for a single region and up to 90 days for multi-region rollouts.

## Self-serve onboarding

Starter and Team customers use in-product onboarding: a guided import of up to 5,000 documents, a checklist in the admin panel, and access to weekly group office hours. No dedicated implementation engineer is assigned.
