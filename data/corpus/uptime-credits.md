# Uptime Credit Claims

## Credit schedule

If monthly availability falls below the commitment, the customer receives a credit against the next invoice: 10 percent of the monthly fee below 99.9 percent and at or above 99.0 percent, 25 percent below 99.0 percent and at or above 98.0 percent, and 50 percent below 98.0 percent. Credits are the sole remedy for missed availability.

## How to claim

Claims must be submitted through the support portal within 30 days of the end of the affected month, using the category Billing, then SLA credit. A claim must include the workspace ID and the observed error rate. Claims submitted after the 30 day window are rejected automatically. Approved credits appear on the invoice issued in the following month.
