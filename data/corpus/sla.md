# Service Level Agreement

## Uptime commitment

Northwind Cloud commits to 99.9 percent monthly availability for the API and web application on Business and Enterprise plans, and 99.5 percent on Starter and Team. Availability is measured per calendar month from the status page, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance.

## What counts as downtime

Downtime means that more than 5 percent of requests across all regions return a 5xx status or take longer than 30 seconds. Errors caused by the customer, by expired credentials, or by requests exceeding documented rate limits are excluded from the calculation. Third-party integrations are not covered by the SLA.
