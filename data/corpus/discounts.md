# Discounts and Nonprofit Pricing

## Volume and term discounts

Discounts are available for annual prepayment (10 percent), three-year commitments (20 percent) and volumes above 250 seats (negotiated). Discounts do not stack; the single largest applicable discount applies.

## Nonprofit and education

Registered nonprofits and accredited educational institutions receive 50 percent off the Team and Business plans after verification, which typically takes 5 business days. The discount applies to subscription fees, not to overage.
