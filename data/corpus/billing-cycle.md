# Billing Cycle and Invoicing

## When you are charged

Subscriptions renew on the first day of each calendar month, regardless of the day the contract started. New accounts are prorated from the activation date to the end of that month and then billed in full. Annual contracts are invoiced upfront and renew on the anniversary of the signature date.

## Invoices and payment methods

Invoices are issued on the second business day of the month and are payable within 30 days. Accepted payment methods are credit card, ACH transfer and wire. Wire transfers under 1,000 USD incur a 25 USD handling fee. Invoices more than 15 days overdue suspend write access to the workspace; invoices more than 45 days overdue are referred to collections and the workspace is set to read-only.
