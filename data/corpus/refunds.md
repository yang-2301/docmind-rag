# Refunds and Cancellations

## Cancelling a subscription

You may cancel at any time from Settings, Billing. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period; access continues until then. Annual contracts that are cancelled before the anniversary date are not refunded for the unused months, except within the first 14 days of the initial term.

## Refund eligibility

Monthly subscriptions are refundable only if the request is made within 7 days of the charge and the account has consumed less than 10 percent of included API calls. Enterprise contracts may include a refund clause in the order form, which overrides this policy. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## Service credits are not refunds

Uptime credits granted under the SLA are applied to future invoices and cannot be converted to cash or refunded. A credit balance expires 12 months after issue if unused.
