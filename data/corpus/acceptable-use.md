# Acceptable Use Policy

## Prohibited content

Customers may not store malware, run denial-of-service tests without written permission, or use the service to process data they are not lawfully entitled to process. Bulk scraping of third-party sites through the service is prohibited.

## Enforcement

Suspected violations are reviewed by the trust and safety team, and the customer is notified within 2 business days unless notification would create legal risk. Repeated violations may lead to suspension of write access.
