# Audit Logs

## Coverage

Audit logs record authentication events, permission changes, document downloads, API key creation and deletion, and configuration changes. Logs are retained for 400 days on Business and Enterprise and 90 days on Starter and Team.

## Access and export

Administrators can view logs in the admin panel and stream them to an external SIEM through the audit log export endpoint. Exported records are immutable; deleting a user does not delete their historical entries.
