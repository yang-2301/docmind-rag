# Supported Integrations

## Native integrations

Native connectors exist for Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Jira, Zendesk, Snowflake, BigQuery and Google Drive. Connectors sync on a schedule of 15 minutes at the fastest; real-time sync is available for Salesforce and HubSpot on Enterprise.

## Custom integrations

Anything not covered natively can be built against the REST API or by subscribing to webhooks. Northwind does not provide custom connector development, but maintains a list of certified implementation partners.
