# Support Plans and Response Times

## Response targets

Support is available 24 hours a day for Enterprise and Business, and 8x5 (09:00 to 18:00 UTC, Monday to Friday) for Starter and Team. First response targets: P1 one hour, P2 four hours, P3 one business day, P4 three business days. These are response targets, not resolution targets.

## Severity definitions

P1 is a production outage with no workaround affecting many users. P2 is degraded functionality with a workaround. P3 is a single-user issue or a question about configuration. P4 is a feature request or documentation fix. Customers assign the severity; Northwind may downgrade a severity after review and must explain why in writing.

## Channels

Tickets can be opened in the support portal, by email to support@northwindcloud.example, or through the Slack Connect channel included with Enterprise. Phone support is reserved for P1 incidents on Enterprise.
